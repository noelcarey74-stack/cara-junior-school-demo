Child Safeguarding

Child Safeguarding Statement & Risk Assessment

Cara Junior School has adopted and follows child safeguarding procedures for schools. The school recognises that child protection and welfare considerations must be reflected in school policies, procedures, practices and activities.

The Child Safeguarding Statement sets out how the school seeks to protect pupils from harm and how it manages child protection concerns in line with relevant school procedures.

Child safeguarding is everyone’s responsibility. All staff and school personnel are expected to act in the best interests of pupils and follow the school’s child protection procedures.

Key Areas

Safeguarding

Protecting pupils and promoting their welfare is central to school life.

The school identifies risks and records procedures to reduce those risks.
